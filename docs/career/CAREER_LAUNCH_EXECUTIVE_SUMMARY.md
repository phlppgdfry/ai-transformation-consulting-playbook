> **Owner-review draft.** Not an approved CV, profile, application or outreach message. Personal facts and contribution require owner confirmation. Vacancy observations retain their original 6 November 2026 date; recheck before use.

Prepared 6 November 2026 from public main evidence, later local showcase proposals and
live employer listings. No application, outreach, profile publication or deployment occurred.

**Strongest positioning today:** a developing business–IT analyst with an AI-assisted,
inspectable portfolio. Primary direction: business/functional analysis and business
applications. Secondary: supported junior AI implementation/automation consulting.

**Present first:** the [Automation Hub flagship](FLAGSHIP_PROJECT_STRATEGY.md). It shows
a business need becoming a visible human-controlled workflow. Use the Playbook as the
business-facing entry and Agent Governance as evidence of critical evaluation, not as
a production-ready model demo.

**Credible demonstration:** synthetic employee submission, deterministic routing,
reasoned human decision, owner-filtered history, repeatable tests, real confirmed HTTP
integration and reproducible financial conclusions. Degree, past work, unaided coding,
client facilitation and personal decisions cannot be inferred from the artifacts.

**Do not claim:** independent authorship of every component, professional Noordwerk client
delivery, real tenant deployment, production identity/security, passed model quality gates,
realised savings, senior architecture/engineering or management responsibility.

**Target roles:** Business Applications Analyst, Functional Analyst, Digital Transformation
Analyst and genuinely junior AI Business Analyst/Implementation Consultant roles. AI
Automation Specialist needs careful scope checking; AI Product Analyst/APM is secondary.
Head of AI, Architect, Director, CTO and Founder are long-term ambitions, not current
qualifications. Verify [actual vacancies and gates](CAREER_OPPORTUNITIES.md) before applying.

**Three skills first:** (1) REST/JSON plus authorization tracing, (2) Python reading/testing,
(3) SQL fundamentals for business questions. Tie each to a task you can explain unaided.
Continue process/acceptance/value reasoning alongside those technical foundations.

**Biggest weakness:** independent understanding and contribution are not yet demonstrated
by the repository evidence alone. Extensive AI assistance is not itself disqualifying;
inability to inspect, reproduce or explain the output would be the risk. Establish what
you actually know through an unaided exercise and honest contribution ledger.

**Stop spending time on:** more frameworks, new portfolio platforms, cosmetic commit
volume, senior titles and a cloud deployment merely to look established. The published
case already supports conversations. The separate local website proposal has existing
lint/dependency issues; it is not a prerequisite to apply with the published repositories.

**Tomorrow:** confirm education/language/licence/commute/start-date facts; rehearse the
five-minute Hub case with no model; complete one API explanation without AI; select one
eligible employer listing and tailor the independent-project CV section. These are
owner actions, not actions already executed by this agent.

**Within 30 days:** follow the [40-hour plan](30_DAY_EXECUTION_PLAN.md): demonstrate the
case twice unaided, produce one captioned recording, complete two mock interviews,
target eight well-matched applications and twelve personalised messages with owner review.
Aim for two substantive conversations; never report those targets as achieved outcomes.
