## Three directions

| Strategy | Credible starting roles | Employability judgment / gaps | Progression and alignment |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — Business–IT bridge | Business Applications Analyst, Functional Analyst, Digital Transformation Analyst; AI Business Analyst where junior scope is explicit | Strongest evidence match: requirements, process/acceptance, workflow and business case. Need independent API/SQL fluency, real stakeholder practice and employer-specific platform learning | Analyst → functional/implementation consultant → solution/product responsibility; strongest current alignment |
| B — AI consulting/transformation | Junior AI Solutions Consultant, supported AI Implementation Consultant, AI Automation Specialist with bounded delivery | Good secondary direction. Portfolio supports controls/evaluation reasoning; production Copilot/Azure/tenant delivery and client facilitation are not proven | Consultant → delivery/solution lead after real implementations, not after another title-rich repo |
| C — AI product/entrepreneurship | AI Product Analyst or Associate Product Manager with mentoring | Useful longer route; need discovery with real users, prioritisation under constraints, instrumentation and outcome ownership. A fictional roadmap is not a shipped product track record | Product analyst/APM → PM → leadership/founder after customer/market and team evidence |

Recommend **A primary**, **B secondary**. Use product thinking inside those roles rather
than making a senior product title the first filter. Live vacancy research is in
[Career Opportunities](CAREER_OPPORTUNITIES.md); these are fit judgments, not hiring probabilities.

## Positioning statements — proposed, not published

- LinkedIn headline: **Business–IT Bridge | Business Analysis & AI Automation Portfolio | Workflow Design, Evaluation & Governance | Belgium**
- CV profile: **Seeking a business/functional analysis or junior AI implementation role. Self-directed, AI-assisted portfolio work connects requirements and process design to a tested local workflow and transparent AI/business-case evaluation.**
- GitHub: **Practical business–IT portfolio work: requirements, local automation, AI evaluation and explicit limitations. Built with substantial AI assistance; source and reproducible checks are open for review.**
- Website: **I explore how business problems become practical software and defensible AI decisions. My Noordwerk portfolio combines process analysis, a working request workflow and model experiments that preserve their failures.**
- Networking: **I am looking for a supervised business–IT role where I can contribute process/requirements thinking while strengthening implementation and evaluation skills.**

Do not use 'Principal', 'Architect', 'Head of AI', 'CTO', 'enterprise expert' or 'production
AI consultant' as current qualifications based on these repositories. Those are ambitions,
not evidence. A career goal can say 'growing toward solution architecture' without implying
current competence at that level.

## Evidence and responsibility boundaries

You can show a working synthetic demo, explain requirements and inspect tests. You cannot
infer mastery from their existence. Before saying 'I designed/implemented X', complete a
ledger: decision, what you contributed, AI assistance, review/correction, reproducible test.
Use 'the portfolio implements' until that personal claim is substantiated. State that
AI materially supported code, documentation and verification; don't minimise it to
'occasional autocomplete'. No model rollout, real customer, measured ROI or Microsoft
tenant deployment is demonstrated by Noordwerk.

Long-term architecture/leadership needs production delivery, operational accountability,
team/customer trust and repeated decisions under real constraints. The next step is a
credible role and supervised delivery, not another large speculative platform.
