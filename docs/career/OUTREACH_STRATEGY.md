# Outreach strategy — drafts only, no messages sent

> **Owner-review draft.** Not an approved CV, profile, application or outreach message. Personal facts and contribution require owner confirmation. Vacancy observations retain their original 6 November 2026 date; recheck before use.

Goal: a relevant conversation about role expectations and fit, not immediate requests
for favours or inflated claims. Use published main links today. Replace every placeholder
with a verified public fact; if none exists, omit the personalisation sentence. No bulk
automation, scraped contact lists, paid ads or unsolicited attachments are necessary.

## Five message templates

### 1. AI consultancy recruiter

Hello [Name], I saw [verified junior/functional role or programme] at [Company]. I'm based
in West Flanders and exploring business–IT roles with supported AI implementation work.
My AI-assisted Noordwerk portfolio connects requirements to a working local approval
workflow and preserves its failed model evaluations. It is independent work, not client
delivery. Would this kind of evidence be useful for your junior/functional hiring route,
and which entry requirements should I check first?

Evidence: [Playbook](https://github.com/phlppgdfry/ai-transformation-consulting-playbook).
For delaware, explicitly mention the advertised September 2027 intake rather than assuming
an immediate opening. Confirm your own degree/language/licence facts before discussing fit.

### 2. AI startup founder

Hello [Name], [specific public product/workflow] caught my attention because it connects
AI to an operational decision. I'm exploring business analysis and implementation roles.
In an independent, AI-assisted fictional case, I kept routing deterministic and examined
why a policy model still failed its gates. What do you most need from someone joining
the business–technical side of a small team? I can share a short working demo if relevant.

Do not imply you used their product or know their customers unless true. A failed model
report can be a useful conversation starter; it is not security certification.

### 3. Product manager

Hello [Name], I noticed [verified product responsibility/public talk]. I'm developing
business–IT skills through a fictional, AI-assisted automation case. The interesting
decision was not adding more AI: the financial base was negative in year one, so the
case recommends a bounded pilot with adoption gates. What evidence would make you trust
an entry-level product/analysis candidate's judgment beyond a polished demo?

### 4. Digital transformation manager

Hello [Name], [verified transformation theme] aligns with the kind of role I'm seeking.
My independent Noordwerk case traces an email request process through requirements,
BPMN, a working human-approval application and an explicit go/no-go business case.
All data is synthetic and the work used substantial AI assistance. Which part would be
most useful to discuss in your team: acceptance criteria, change/adoption, or technical
feasibility? I'd appreciate a short perspective if your schedule allows.

### 5. Enterprise IT manager

Hello [Name], I'm exploring business applications/functional analysis roles in Belgium.
I have a small AI-assisted reference case with a .NET request API, human decisions,
retry/persistence tests and clear production limitations. It uses public demo identities,
not a deployed tenant. What practical API, data and acceptance-testing skills do you
expect from someone entering the business–IT boundary? I can point to one relevant artifact
rather than sending a large portfolio.

## Follow-up and response handling

One follow-up after 7–10 working days if appropriate: 'Hello [Name], one brief follow-up.
Would a five-minute workflow demo or the requirements-to-test map be more relevant to
your team? No problem if the timing is not right.' Then stop unless invited. Do not
assume silence means a skills judgment, and never claim a relationship from a connection.

If someone replies: answer their question, offer the one requested artifact and respect
their process. For an advertised role, use the formal application route; networking does
not replace it. Don't send confidential code, real records or unnecessary private details.

## Zero-advertising-budget weekly routine

Identify three relevant people through an official role, product page or a real event
you choose to attend. Read one public item from each. Draft two or three short messages;
the owner reviews and sends only if appropriate. Offer a concrete question or observation,
not 'please mentor me'. Keep private notes outside public GitHub. Target useful replies,
not follower count. Use existing employer events only after independently checking current
date/cost/access; no unverified free event is promised here.

Nothing in this document authorizes an agent to contact recipients, publish a post,
submit an application, follow accounts or sign up for alerts. Those actions remain with
the owner unless separately and explicitly delegated.
